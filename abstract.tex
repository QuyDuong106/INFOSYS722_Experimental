This study compares interpretable and ensemble classifiers for existing gallstone status using 319 records and 38 candidate predictors. Leave-one-patient-out internal validation was implemented as record-level leave-one-out under a one-record-per-person assumption. Historical fixed and nested Optuna comparisons used training-fitted recursive CART selection of twenty source features. An updated stacking experiment jointly tuned XGBoost, LightGBM and ridge base learners and feature count, retaining a fixed logistic meta-learner. Each excluded-record search used twenty trials and three inner validation folds. Updated stacking achieved pooled ROC--AUC 0.876, accuracy 0.806, sensitivity 0.839, specificity 0.772 and F1 0.813 across 319 held-out predictions, missing 26 of 161 cases. All 38 predictors were selected in 239 outer folds. Historical tuned logistic regression achieved AUC 0.868; its fold-specific models were examined with SHAP and LIME. Changed model composition and search design prevent attributing the stacking improvement to a single component. Prior cohort exploration, retrospective model comparison and absent external validation limit inference. The findings concern existing disease status and do not establish prospective prediction or clinical utility.
