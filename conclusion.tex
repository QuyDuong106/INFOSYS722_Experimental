The updated base-tuned stacking experiment achieved pooled LOPO AUC 0.876, accuracy 0.806 and sensitivity 0.839, with 26 false negatives among 161 cases. It jointly tuned XGBoost, LightGBM and ridge bases and RFE feature count while retaining a fixed logistic meta-learner; all 38 fields were selected in most exclusions. These are the highest observed discrimination and F1 among the completed tuned experiments, but the changed architecture and search design preclude attributing improvement to a single component. Historical tuned logistic models remain the subject of the SHAP/LIME analysis. Training-specific preparation and nested validation support reproducibility, while prior exploration, retrospective comparisons and absent external validation restrict the findings to internal classification of existing gallstone status.
