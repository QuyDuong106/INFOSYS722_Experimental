Under a shared LOPO protocol, logistic regression provided the strongest probability discrimination, random forest offered the highest sensitivity and accuracy, and stacking achieved the highest specificity. These results support an interpretable baseline and explicit reporting of classification trade-offs rather than a single performance ranking. Fold-specific preparation and one held-out prediction per record provide a reproducible internal comparison. The small single-source cohort, prior dataset exploration, absent calibration, and lack of external validation restrict the findings to comparative research on existing gallstone status.
