\textcite{esen2024early} investigated gallstone classification using bioimpedance and laboratory data; the released dataset forms the empirical basis of this study \parencite{uci2025gallstone}. \textcite{salem2023ml} also examined machine-learning associations between gallstone disease, clinical features, and metabolites. Together these studies motivate examining multivariable information, although differences in samples and evaluation protocols preclude treating their reported scores as directly comparable benchmarks.

Random forests provide an ensemble alternative to a linear classifier \parencite{breiman2001randomforests}. The present comparison additionally includes gradient boosting and probability stacking to assess whether more flexible models improve on logistic regression in this small tabular sample. The implementation uses scikit-learn for preprocessing and baseline estimation \parencite{pedregosa2011scikit}; continuous measurements undergo Yeo--Johnson transformation \parencite{yeo2000new}. The contribution is a comparative internal evaluation and feature-selection analysis, rather than a new classification algorithm or an independent replication cohort.
