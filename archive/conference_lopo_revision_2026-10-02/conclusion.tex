Logistic regression provided the strongest validation discrimination across two CART-based selectors and was selected in every holdout comparison. Random forest offered higher sensitivity in complementary leave-one-out evaluation, while reducing the RFE feature count from 20 to 10 weakened mean validation performance. These results support an interpretable baseline and explicit reporting of error trade-offs. Reused internal partitions, absent calibration, and lack of external validation restrict the findings to comparative research on this dataset.
